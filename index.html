How to Get Started — Course Access Guide

Follow these three simple steps to begin your learning journey.

01

Register or log in

Open the academy course portal and sign in with your registered account. If you are a new learner, complete the registration process first.

02

Find your enrolled course

Visit your course dashboard and choose the course you have enrolled in, such as MS Excel, IT Skills, English Communication, or Mathematics.

03

Click “Start Learning”

Open your lessons, watch the learning videos, complete the activities, and practise regularly to build real-world skills.
